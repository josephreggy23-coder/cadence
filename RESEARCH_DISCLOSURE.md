# Research disclosure

CADENCE was developed with software-assistance tools used for code review,
debugging, documentation editing, and methodological checks. The project author
is responsible for every scientific decision, interpretation, and submitted
statement.

The H1R recordings are public data from Taylor et al.; their provenance and
citations are documented in `docs/real_data.md` and `docs/references.md`.

Before using this work in a competition or publication, verify the applicable
authorship and software-disclosure rules, reproduce every reported result, and
write the submitted narrative independently.
